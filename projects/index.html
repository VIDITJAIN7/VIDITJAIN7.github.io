02 / Fractal

Search that combines meaning with exactness.

Fractal pairs Gemini embeddings with Elasticsearch full-text search, then fuses both signals with Reciprocal Rank Fusion. The result is a search experience that can understand intent without losing precise keyword matches.

04 / Data Poisoning Detection Pipeline

Testing whether a model can spot its own bad data.

On CIFAR-10 with ResNet18, I compared Spectral Signatures and Activation Clustering for backdoor and poisoning detection. The pipeline uses AMP and TF32 for faster experimentation and recorded a 1.22% attack success rate in the evaluated configuration.
