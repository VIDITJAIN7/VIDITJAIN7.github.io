QUANTUM COMPUTING · SEPTEMBER 2026—PRESENT

Defense against circuit-level threats in quantum federated learning

Advised by Dr. Ashish Gupta · BITS Pilani Dubai Campus

QiskitPennyLaneQuantum federated learning

How can a federated quantum model remain reliable when a participant’s circuit has been manipulated?

I’m studying CULT attacks that inject unitary operators into parameterized quantum circuits, including Pauli rotations and phase-kickback sign flips. The work examines state deviations and shifts in measurement expectations, then investigates robust aggregation to limit global model drift.

Research approach

The analysis considers attacker stealth-budget constraints alongside changes in circuit states and model updates. This is ongoing research; evaluation results will be added as the work develops.

BAYESIAN INFERENCE · 2026

Nowcasting migration
in the UAE.

Advised by Dr. Mrutuyanjaya Sahu

PyMCNUTS samplingAR(1) temporal smoothing

Combining delayed administrative records with digital traces to estimate the present.

The report covers 54 migration corridors from 2015–2024. It combines FCSC records, the UAE Labour Force Survey, UN DESA benchmarks, and Meta Marketing API signals in a Bayesian hierarchical model, accounting for source-specific bias.

Data, validation & limitations

The report records a 64% reduction in RMSE against its baseline. Administrative sources can lag by 12–24 months; faster digital signals require correction for platform penetration and source bias.

The credible interval is central to the result. Digital traces are proxies, and the model’s estimates should be read alongside their uncertainty.

EXPLAINABLE AI · 2026

Understanding congestion
across Dubai’s transit network.

Counterfactual explanations · Advised by Dr. Mrutuyanjaya Sahu

GCN-GRUNSGA-IIRTA / Open-Meteo / OSM

Forecasting congestion, then asking which operational changes could reduce it.

A GCN-GRU model uses 8,784 hourly observations from 2024 across 29 metro stations and six bus hubs. NSGA-II searches for changes in parking capacity, bus frequency, service headway, and fare class against a target of 30% lower peak congestion.

Findings & model caveats

The counterfactual search suggests increasing feeder-bus frequency at BurJuman (+80%), Union (+60%), and Al Rigga (+55%). These are model recommendations, not implemented changes or observed improvements.

The report flags counterintuitive headway increases as a model artifact requiring transport-domain validation. The 30% reduction is a search target, not a measured real-world result.
