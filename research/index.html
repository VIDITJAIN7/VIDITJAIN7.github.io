01 / IN PROGRESS · 09/2026—PRESENT

Defense Against Circuit-Level Threats in Quantum Federated Learning

With advisor Dr. Ashish Gupta, I’m examining how attacks that target quantum circuits can undermine federated learning. The study is focused on threat modelling, robust detection, and the gap between an algorithm that performs well in isolation and one that remains trustworthy when collaborators or circuits are adversarial.

02 / COMPLETED STUDY · 02—10/2026

UAE Migration Nowcasting

With Dr. Mrutuyanjaya Sahu, I built a Bayesian nowcasting approach for estimating migration flows across 54 corridors from 2015–2024. The work combines delayed official indicators with digital-trace proxies and keeps uncertainty visible instead of presenting a single overconfident number.

Big data analyticsPyMC / NUTSAR(1) time seriesPolicy analytics
Finding. The model estimated 10.86M migrants, with an 80% credible interval of 8.25M–14.71M and under 1.2% MAPE on held-out validation.

03 / COMPLETED STUDY · 02—10/2026

Counterfactual Explanations for Dubai Public Transportation

This study investigates how to explain and improve public transport performance at network level. A graph-convolutional GRU model was trained on 8,784 hourly observations from 2024 across a 35-node network of metro stations and bus hubs, then paired with counterfactual recommendations.

GCN-GRUCounterfactual MLUrban analytics35-node graph
Model result. R² 0.9580 and RMSE 0.0434 at epoch 88. Recommendations target a 30% reduction in delay, with headway effects treated as a modelling caveat.
