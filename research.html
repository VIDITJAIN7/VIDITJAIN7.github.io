QUANTUM COMPUTING · 2026–PRESENT

Defense against circuit-level threats in quantum federated learning

Current research with Dr. Ashish Gupta at BITS Pilani Dubai Campus.

Developing countermeasures against CULT attacks that inject unitary operators — including Pauli rotations and phase-kickback sign flips — into parameterized quantum circuits. The work studies state deviations and measurement expectation shifts, then uses robust aggregation to limit global model drift under attacker stealth-budget constraints.

EXPLAINABLE AI · DUBAI · 2026

AI-assisted analysis of public transportation performance

A report on congestion across Dubai’s Metro Red Line using forecasting plus counterfactual explanations.

A GCN-GRU model was trained on 8,784 hourly observations from 2024, representing a 35-node network of 29 metro stations and 6 bus hubs. NSGA-II then searched for minimal operational changes — parking capacity, bus frequency, service headway, and fare class — that could target a 30% reduction from documented peak congestion.

BAYESIAN INFERENCE · UAE · 2026

Bayesian nowcasting of UAE migration using social media proxies

A demographic nowcasting framework combining administrative records with high-frequency digital traces.

The study fuses FCSC administrative data, the UAE Labour Force Survey, UN DESA benchmarks, and Meta Marketing API signals across 54 migration corridors from 2015–2024. A PyMC Bayesian hierarchical model uses NUTS sampling and AR(1) temporal smoothing to estimate source-specific bias instead of treating any single dataset as ground truth.

MIGRATION / MODEL NOTES

Why the fusion matters.

The model is designed around the gaps in each source.

01

Delayed registers

FCSC records can lag the present by 12–24 months and show an estimated 8% undercount against the Labour Force Survey.

02

Digital traces

Meta active-user signals provide a faster proxy, but require learned correction for platform penetration and source bias.

03

Uncertainty, exposed

Corridor-level credible intervals make the uncertainty visible instead of hiding it behind one national estimate.
